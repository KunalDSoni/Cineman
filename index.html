Chapter 03

Not a
Production House.
A Cinematic
Universe.

CineMan was born in 2010 when three storytellers — Abhishek Jain, Mikhil Musale, and Anish Shah — decided that Gujarati cinema deserved to be taken seriously.

What began as a regional experiment became a national reckoning. Wrong Side Raju won the National Award. OHO Gujarati built a streaming platform for an entire language. And now, CineMan channels that same storytelling precision into premium branded content for brands that want to move people.
