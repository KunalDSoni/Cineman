01 — Brand Principles

HOW WE THINK

01

Cinema is the highest form of strategy.

Every creative decision — from color to cut to copy — is made with the same intentionality as a screenplay. Nothing decorative. Everything purposeful. The design serves the story, and the story serves the feeling we want to leave behind.

02

Warmth before cool.

The yellow-black palette is a deliberate rejection of cold modernism. Cinema is warm. Memory is amber. The tungsten glow of a projector is the warmest light in any room. Our visual identity must always feel like entering that room.

03

Regional is not a limitation. It is a superpower.

CineMan was born in Ahmedabad. That specificity is the brand's greatest asset. The most universal stories are the most specific ones. We do not hide our roots — we project them onto every screen we touch.

04

Every scroll is a scene change.

The website is not a brochure. It is a film. Each section is a scene. Each transition is a cut. The user is not browsing — they are watching. Design and motion must honor that contract.

05

Silence is part of the composition.

White space — or in our case, dark space — is not emptiness. It is dramatic pause. The gap between words is where meaning lives. Never fill for the sake of filling. Let the frame breathe.

06 — Motion System

CINEMATIC
MOTION

Every animation is a directorial choice. Inspired by Monks.com's motion hierarchy — deliberate, weighty, emotionally calibrated. GSAP + Lenis + film-inspired easing.

◈
Cinematic Loader

Film countdown (5→1), filmstrip holes, progress bar, logo reveal. Sets the cinematic tone before a single scroll.

duration: 2.2s · ease: power2.inOut
⊙
Hero Word Reveal

Clip-path inset(0 0 100% → 0) per word, staggered 180ms. Feels like a title card appearing from darkness.

stagger: 0.18s · ease: power4.out · clip-path
◎
Custom Cursor

Dual-ring cursor — tight dot (lerp 0.9) + lagging ring (lerp 0.14). Expands on hover, shows VIEW label on film cards.

lerp: 0.9 / 0.14 · magnetic: ±25px
◇
Scroll Parallax

Hero background drifts at 15% slower than scroll rate. Creates depth without being distracting. Lenis + ScrollTrigger scrub.

scrub: 1.5 · yPercent: 15 · ease: none
◐
Film Grain

Canvas-based animated noise at 4.5% opacity, mix-blend-mode: overlay. Runs at 60fps. Gives the screen the texture of film stock.

opacity: 0.045 · blend: overlay · 60fps canvas
◉
Magnetic Buttons

Primary CTAs magnetically attract to cursor within a 30% offset radius. Elastic snap-back on mouseleave.

offset: ×0.25 · elastic.out(1, 0.4) · 0.8s
◫
Card Tilt

Film and director cards respond to mouse with subtle 3D perspective tilt (max ±6°). Resets with elastic spring.

rotateX/Y: ±6° · perspective: 800 · elastic.out
◬
Section Reveals

Each section element animates in from below (y: 30px → 0) or left/right on ScrollTrigger. Staggered for groups.

y: 30px → 0 · once: true · power3.out · 0.9s
◭
Manifesto Words

Each word in the manifesto reveals individually with 60ms stagger — as if being typed by a projectionist.

stagger: 60ms · opacity: 0→1 · translateY: 40→0
◮
Marquee Speed

Award/title marquee reverses direction on scroll-up. Slight acceleration on fast scroll. Pause on hover (via CSS).

animation: 30s linear infinite · scroll-aware
◯
Light Leak Effect

Hero light leak follows mouse position at 30% offset, creating the illusion of a real projector responding to presence.

gsap.to: x/y mouse×0.3 · duration: 2s · power1.out
◰
Film Burn Transition

On high-velocity scroll, a subtle amber flash overlay fires — evoking the physical film burn between projector reels.

opacity: velocity/300 · maxOpacity: 0.12 · 100ms flash
07 — Case Study Architecture

HOW WE
TELL WORK

Every case study is a short film about a film. It should feel editorial, cinematic, and emotionally resonant — not a bullet-point portfolio piece.

01
The Cinematic Intro

Hero image/reel, film title card format, key metadata (director, year, format, recognition). Sets scene before context.

02
The Insight

What truth drove this project? Not "the brief" — the human insight. The thing that made this worth making at all.

03
Visual Language

How did we light it? What palette? What framing grammar? The aesthetic decisions and the reasoning behind them.

04
Production Philosophy

Casting, location, rehearsal, the unexpected discoveries. The process, told with the candor of a director's commentary.

05
The Pull Quote

One line from the director, editor, or client that captures what the film achieved. In Playfair italic. Gold. Full bleed.

06
Impact Storytelling

Not metrics first. Cultural impact first. Awards, conversations started, audiences moved. Data follows feeling.
